How it trains you

From "Start" to certification-ready

A focused practice loop you can run as many times as you want — every retest reshuffles a fresh 60 questions, renumbered 1–60.

1

Sign in & start

Open http://localhost:8095. The 90-minute timer starts automatically. Pause anytime; pick up where you left off.

2

Answer & learn as you go

Submit each answer to see if you got it right, the correct choice(s), an explanation paragraph, and the official reference link.

3

Finish test → in-page results

Big score number + breakdown tiles: Completed, Correct Incorrect, Unanswered. No popups.

4

Drill into your mistakes

Click Correct or Incorrect to open a full-width review — 3 questions per page with your answer, the right answer, and the explanation. Ideal for targeted study.

5

Retest with one click

"Retest" reshuffles a fresh 60 from the bank and resets the timer. Or reset just the timer if you want another 90 minutes on the same set.
